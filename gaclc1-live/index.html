What this live surface proves

It proves the interface architecture, deterministic virtual mission flow, acceptance-gate model, public source mirror, and direct evidence routing.

It does not prove a commissioned physical data center, measured field water savings, certified coolant chemistry, hardware uptime, or independent engineering approval. Those claims remain locked until bench, rack, pod, hall, and field gates produce calibrated evidence.
